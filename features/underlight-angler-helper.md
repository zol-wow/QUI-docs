# Underlight Angler Helper

The Underlight Angler is the Legion fishing artifact. Getting it takes a short chain of prerequisites, and Blizzard's artifact window no longer draws its trait tree. This optional module tracks the unlock for you and gives the tree back.

It is a port of the standalone Underlight Angler UI addon by CoreMeeko2, brought into QUI's look and feel at the author's request.

## How to Enable

The module ships enabled. Turn it off or on under `/qui` > **Module Addons** > **Underlight Angler Helper**.

Open the window with `/angler` or `/quiangler`. While the Underlight Angler artifact is open at the Luminous Pearl in Dalaran, an **Underlight Angler Tree** button also appears on Blizzard's artifact window.

If the standalone Underlight Angler UI addon is also enabled, it keeps `/angler` and its own artifact-window button; use `/quiangler` for the QUI window.

## Checklist

The first tab shows the four steps in the order the game enforces them and tells you which one is next:

1. The **Bigger Fish to Fry** achievement on this character.
2. **Legion Fishing** at 100.
3. The **Luminous Pearl** questline.
4. The **Underlight Angler** itself.

When the questline sends you to Khadgar, the tab shows a map of Dalaran with his real location inside The Violet Citadel circled. The quest marker on the world map points somewhere else.

The checklist updates as you play. If the Legion Fishing skill shows as unavailable, open the Legion Fishing page of your profession window and press **Refresh**.

## Artifact Tree

The second tab draws the twelve traits and the links between them.

- Red links are the route to buy first: water walking and swim speed.
- Yellow links are the second route: reduced threat while fishing.
- Each trait shows its rank. Hover it for the next rank's cost and whether its prerequisites are met.

Ranks and Artifact Power are only readable while the Underlight Angler artifact is open, so open it at the Luminous Pearl first. Click a trait to buy its next rank; QUI asks for confirmation before spending. Traits cannot be bought in combat.

## Tips

The third tab collects shortcuts for repeating the unlock on an alt: where to level Legion Fishing, how to farm Artifact Power in Azsuna, rough Artifact Power targets, and what to check when water walking does not work after buying the traits.
